## What we do
Ultrastable is an autonomous system that sets up and then keeps running and improving an organized effort from one idea. The effort can be a company, an open-source project, a community or a lab. It does the routine work with AI managers and staff, and only steps that cannot be undone come to a person.

## Where we are
Early. It is working today on a small number of the founder's own projects. There are no outside customers yet and the team is one founder.
