## 1. Describe what your company does in 50 characters or less.
_(secondary, 39/50 characters)_

AI managers that keep systems improving

## 2. What is your company going to make? Please describe your product and what it does or will do.
_(verified, 706/1500 characters)_

Ultrastable is the management layer after the first version. Coding agents and app builders produce a first draft and then wait to be told what to do next. Ultrastable runs AI managers (portfolio, product, project) that read the system's own measurements, decide what to improve, file the work, hand it to coding agents, prove each change live with a probe, and keep score. A person states the idea, the measure and a monthly budget; the system supplies the backlog and closes its own loops. It runs on Cloudflare Workers. The product runs live at ultrastable.io, with a public read-only MCP endpoint at ultrastable.io/mcp. Ultrastable runs its own roadmap, issues and deploys with the same code it offers.

## 3. Why did you pick this idea to work on? Do you have domain expertise in this area? How do you know people need what you're making?
_(secondary, 367/1500 characters)_

I am an ideas person. I kept starting systems that needed someone to run them after launch, and every tool I tried stopped at the first draft. So I built the managers I needed, and they run my own projects. The need is a hypothesis we are testing: there are no outside customers yet, and the next step is pilots with people who run something that must keep improving.

## 4. What's new about what you're making? What substitutes do people resort to because it doesn't exist yet?
_(secondary, 382/1500 characters)_

What is new is that the system decides the next piece of work itself, from its own measures, and must prove each change live before it counts. Today people substitute a hired engineer, an agency retainer, or a coding agent they must direct task by task. Ultrastable itself runs on at least 70 registered closed loops, each with a trigger, an owner, a done check and a closing probe.

## 5. Who are your competitors? What do you understand about your business that they don't?
_(verified, 429/1500 characters)_

GitHub Copilot coding agent, OpenAI Codex, Claude Code, Factory and OpenHands execute tasks someone wrote. Lovable, Bolt and Replit build the first version. LangGraph is a framework to build agents. We track at least 8 competitor profiles refreshed by code. What we understand: the bottleneck is not writing code, it is knowing what to ask for next and proving it worked. Whoever owns the backlog and the proof owns the customer.
