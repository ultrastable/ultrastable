# Plussing

"Plussing" is the practice of building on work rather than only critiquing it: every review must add something.

Automated teams tend to devolve into a queue of fixes. Ultrastable counters that structurally:

1. **Every closed item proposes a plus**: one concrete next step that would make the result better, filed with its expected value.
2. **Product managers rank pluses against the roadmap**, so improvement competes for capacity as planned work.
3. **Reserved capacity**: a configurable share of each cycle goes to accepted pluses, not fixes.
4. **Drift detection**: the share of fix-only cycles is measured. When fixes crowd out progress for too long, the portfolio manager rebalances and the cause is filed as its own item.
5. **Reviews are additive**: a reviewer's output includes at least one plus, not only defects.
