# Comparables (brief, 2026-11-11)

Method: two web searches; result lists were read at title level only, not full articles. Treat as orientation, to be deepened by the product manager. No claims below rely on specific figures.

## Product category
- AI app and site builders for non-coders (Lovable, Bolt, Replit, Base44, v0, Squarespace-style builders): comparison write-ups exist for each (e.g. [DEV Community top-5 builders](https://dev.to/8080_ai/top-5-ai-website-builders-in-2026-3p1p), [HostAdvice Lovable vs Bolt vs Replit](https://hostadvice.com/ai-app-builders/lovable-vs-bolt-vs-replit/), [kingy.ai guide for non-developers](https://kingy.ai/news/best-ai-coding-tools-for-non-developers-in-2026-an-honest-guide/)). Pattern: friendly front end, bounded by the platform.
- Code agents (Claude Code, Codex, Copilot): unbounded, assume engineers.
- Gap Ultrastable targets: guided specification and management for people who do not know what to ask, producing code and specs they own.

## Licensing precedent (source-available, non-commercial free, commercial paid)
Elastic License v2, SSPL, Business Source License, Commons Clause, PolyForm Noncommercial, Fair Source ([FOSSA guide](https://fossa.com/blog/comprehensive-guide-source-available-software-licenses/), [Heather Meeker scorecard](https://heathermeeker.com/2018/12/15/source-available-scorecard/), [Elastic ELv2](https://www.elastic.co:443/blog/elastic-license-v2), [Keygen Fair Source](https://keygen.sh/blog/keygen-is-now-fair-source/)). These are source-available, not open source; some vendors reverted after community backlash. Lesson: say "source-available" plainly, never "open source". PolyForm Noncommercial is a lawyer-drafted template the attorney may prefer to adapt.
