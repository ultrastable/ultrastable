# Licensing guide (plain language)

> Working draft for attorney review. Not legal advice. The binding text is [LICENSE](LICENSE) (Ultrastable Hybrid License, UHL, v0.9, `LicenseRef-Ultrastable-Hybrid-0.9`). If this guide and the LICENSE differ, the LICENSE wins.

**Ultrastable is the foundation for any autonomous system.** The code and specifications are source-available: free for non-commercial use, licensed for any use that makes or saves money. There is no patent. The protection is copyright plus this contract, the same hybrid approach as the QNFO User License Agreement.

## What you may do for free

| You are | Free? |
|---|---|
| An individual running it for yourself, earning nothing from it | Yes |
| A teacher, student or researcher (results published) | Yes |
| A registered non-profit, school or government body, for its public mission | Yes |
| A free, unmonetized community or open project built on it | Yes |

## What needs a Commercial License (anywhere in the world)

| You are | Licence needed? |
|---|---|
| A company using it internally, even only to evaluate or test | Yes (LICENSE 3.2(d)) |
| Selling, hosting or reselling it, or offering it as a paid or ad-funded service | Yes (3.2(a), (b)) |
| A consultant or agency using it for client work you are paid for | Yes (3.2(c)) |
| Building a product from the specifications without copying the code | Yes (3.2(f), 3.6) |
| Training or evaluating a commercial AI model on it | Yes (3.2(h)) |
| A nonprofit that later sells it or makes it the paid part of a product | Yes, for that use (3.3) |

The default Commercial License is a **revenue share**: Licensor gets a percentage of the money the use earns. Published defaults, still hypotheses until the owner sets them: [COMMERCIAL-LICENSING.md](COMMERCIAL-LICENSING.md).

## If you share or change it
Keep the notices, pass on this license, include the source, credit Ultrastable, and offer the source of your changes to the users of a hosted copy (LICENSE section 5).

## Why not a patent
The system has already been used in public through QNFO, which makes a patent unlikely to succeed. Rowan decided on 2026-11-11 to drop the patent route and protect the work with copyright, this license and the commercial license instead. The earlier patent drafts are archived under `ip/` (see [ip/README.md](ip/README.md)) and are not deleted.

## Where the license applies
Every repository of the split (see [docs/REPO-SPLIT.md](docs/REPO-SPLIT.md)) carries the same `LICENSE`. Files may add `SPDX-License-Identifier: LicenseRef-Ultrastable-Hybrid-0.9`. `QNFO` content stays under the QNFO ULA.
