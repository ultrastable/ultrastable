# The business manager's view

*Voice: the virtual business and strategy manager. Owns the business model, the plan, and keeping the other managers pointed at the same goal.*

## The verdict in one paragraph

Ultrastable is a business, and the timing is right, but the moat is not "AI agents that run a company". Several products already say that (see [competition](03-competition.md)). The moat is that Ultrastable's agents **earn** their independence from a verified track record, **plan improvement** instead of sliding into endless bug fixing, and **interrupt people only** for steps that are both irreversible and tied to someone's identity. QNFO already runs this way, which makes Rowan's own fleet customer zero and the best evidence there is. The plan is: protect it (file), prove it (case study from QNFO), sell it to people who run many projects with few people, and fund it with credits and a grant before giving up equity.

## What we sell

**The management layer for AI workers.** Coding agents (Devin, Cursor, Claude Code, Codex) are workers. Ultrastable is the manager above them: it keeps the backlog, assigns work, checks results independently, decides how much freedom each agent gets, and reserves time for making things better.

- **Product:** a hosted service plus a portable core. Cloudflare first, Google Cloud second (platform-agnostic per Rowan, 2026-11-10).
- **Package:** the **org kit**: a new organization or project starts with the same roles, gates, backlog shape and review rhythm in one step.
- **Who pays:** people who run several projects with few humans: solo founders, agencies, small engineering teams ([customers](02-customers.md)).

| Solo | One founder, up to 3 projects | about $99 per month + model usage at cost | Cofounder.co lists $120/month and NanoCorp $30/month ([source](https://pancake.ai/blog/best-autonomous-company-platform-2026)) |
| Team | Agency or team, up to 20 projects | about $499 per month + usage | Priced on projects managed, not seats, since the point is fewer seats |
| Enterprise | Larger orgs, own cloud | annual contract | Private deployment, audit export, SSO |

Open core is decided: the code is open and the Ultrastable License governs use.

## Biggest risks

| Risk | What we do about it |
|---|---|
| Others copy the open design | The Ultrastable License requires a Commercial License for commercial use; speed, the running record and the org kit are the moat |
| Big platforms add "trust-based autonomy" themselves | Speed, the licence, the running record and the org kit: we sell a way of running work, not a feature |
| Solo founder | The roster below is the team; first hire is a founding engineer or a commercial co-founder once funded |
| Agents doing harm in a customer's systems | The narrow gate, full audit log, customer-set limits; contract terms |
| Model costs eat margin | Pass usage through at cost; route work to the cheapest model that passes review |
