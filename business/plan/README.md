## Needs a professional

- **[ACCOUNTANT]** Entity and tax election where a programme requires one (most US investors and accelerators expect a Delaware C-corp), R&D tax credit, SBIR grant accounting, how cloud credits and founder expenses are booked.
- Trademark search for "Ultrastable" (note: "ultrastability" is W. Ross Ashby's 1952 term, and agent.ceo markets "cybernetic organizations").
- Customer contract and privacy terms are drafted from the Ultrastable License and the commercial terms; an investment instrument (SAFE or note) follows the investor's own standard form.
