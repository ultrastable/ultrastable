# Marketing strategy (short form)

Source of record for marketing is [../investor/marketing-strategy.md](../investor/marketing-strategy.md). This short form keeps the positioning; if the two differ, the investor file wins and this one is corrected.

Builds on [../plan/06-promotion.md](../plan/06-promotion.md), [../plan/07-events.md](../plan/07-events.md), [../plan/03-competition.md](../plan/03-competition.md). Figures and dates are estimates to verify.

## Positioning
**The self-governing layer around the coding agents a team already uses. Not another agent; not just a gateway.** Category: autonomous development platform. Contrast: vibe coding (you pick every task), cloud coding agents (you assign tasks), control planes and gateways (cost, access, audit). Differentiator, short form: "it runs the project and only interrupts you for what can't be undone." Mechanism form: scored predictions and an earned-trust ladder, open under the Ultrastable License. Alternative framing for the narrative: "I'm an ideas person; I built a manager so I wouldn't have to be one."

## Audiences
| Audience | Need | Message | Channel |
|---|---|---|---|
| Solo founders and ideas people | Ship without being the manager | Start from one idea file | Founder communities, Show HN, YouTube demo |
| Engineering leads with many services | Agents keep services healthy | Fewer hand-backs, full record | Meetups, LinkedIn, newsletter |
| Teams owing auditors an account | Evidence of what agents did | Every decision recorded | Essays, compliance-adjacent communities |
| Funders (credits, SBIR, angels, pre-seed) | Credible, honest early company | Working system, honest gaps | Applications, warm intros |
| Cloud partners (Cloudflare, Google) | Portfolio stories | Portable, runs on both | Startup programs, showcases |

## Phases
1. **Now:** 10 discovery calls and applications with `external/` wording; listener in communities; weekly claims check of the site and repositories.
2. **Launch week:** site update with the licence line, QNFO case study (labeled self-scored), founder post, Show HN and Product Hunt on separate days, 2-minute demo of Ultrastable running its own roadmap.
3. **Steady state:** 3 build-in-public posts a week (the managers' weekly four-line report), 2 essays a month (management bottleneck, earned autonomy, plussing, Ashby), 1 demo video a month, weekly newsletter.

## Events

## Metrics
Pilot requests per week; design partners signed (target 3); site visits from outside the fleet; applications submitted and accepted; newsletter subscribers; weekly report cadence kept; **honesty metric: zero claims that outrun the status labels** (reviewed monthly by the portfolio manager).

## Guardrails
Every number labeled self-reported until audited. No claims of customers, revenue or certifications that do not exist. Owner-only: entity, domain email, submitting applications, sending anything to a third party.
