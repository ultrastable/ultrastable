# Pitch deck

One slide per section. Two variants of slides 8 and 9 for the two routes. No numbers about the product or finances.

**1. Title.** Ultrastable. An autonomous system that turns an idea into a running organization.

**2. Problem.** AI agents can now do real work. A person still has to choose every task, check every result and notice every drift. Agents are cheap; managing them is the bottleneck. Ideas die in the overhead of product, brand, legal protection, search presence and marketing.

**3. Solution.** Ultrastable is the management layer for agent work. Give it an idea; it sets up the roles, plans the work, does it, checks it and reports. You steer when you want. Otherwise it manages itself.

**4. How it feels.** Four lines a day: what happened, what it decided, what is next, what is open. It only interrupts you for decisions that cannot be undone. It gets more independent as it proves itself. It plans improvement, not just fixes. Nothing drops silently.

**5. Why now.** Models can do the work. What is missing is who decides what is missing, who checks, and who reports without burdening the founder. Coding agents write code; Ultrastable runs the system around the code.

**6. Proof.** We run our own work on it. One production installation, self-reported, used as the first test case. We are looking for design partners to run 30-day pilots. (No customer or revenue claims.)

**7. Market and customers.** Founders and small teams with more ideas than staff; agencies and studios running many projects; organizations adopting agents who need them managed. First wedge: solo founders and small teams. (Sizing to be done by the business manager, marked estimate when it exists.)

**8a. Commercial path.** Hosted service per venture, usage-based model costs passed through (planning assumption). Enterprise: runs in the customer's own cloud.
**8b. Open path.** An open core anyone can run, governed openly at ultrastable.org; hosted service and support as the sustaining layer.

**9a. Ask (investor).** Conversation about a pre-seed round when ready; cloud credits; design partners; introductions to an operating co-founder.
**9b. Ask (grant / foundation).** Support to harden and document the open core, security review, and community onboarding; fiscal hosting.

**10. Team.** Rowan: inventor and product direction. The system runs much of its own operation. Seeking: business co-founder or CEO, founding engineer.

**11. Platform stance.** Runs on any cloud: Cloudflare first, Google Cloud next, self-hosted always possible. No lock-in.

**12. Honest limits.** Early. One installation. No financial back-end yet. The code is open; the Ultrastable License governs use.

**13. Close.** Coding agents write code. Ultrastable runs the system.
