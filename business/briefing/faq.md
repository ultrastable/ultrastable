# FAQ (SAFE NOW)

**What is Ultrastable?** An autonomous system that takes an idea and runs the work of building and operating it, with a person steering when they choose.

**How is it different from coding agents?** They write code. Ultrastable decides what needs doing, checks the results and reports. It manages agents rather than being one.

**How is it different from other "AI runs your company" products?** Several exist. Ours is about management quality: it interrupts only for what can't be undone, becomes more independent as it proves itself, and plans improvement rather than only repairing. Ask for a pilot and judge on results.

**Does it replace people?** It removes overhead for small teams and founders. A person still sets direction and takes the legal, financial and identity steps.

**What does it cost?** Not priced yet. Planning assumption: a subscription per venture plus pass-through model costs.

**Is it open source?** Plan: an open core at ultrastable.org with a hosted service as the commercial layer. Timing and licence follow professional review.

**Where does it run?** On the cloud of your choice: Cloudflare first, Google Cloud next, self-hosted as an option. Your own keys are supported.

**Local or hosted agents?** Both are planned. Hosted is the easy default; self-hosting is for those who need data and keys to stay with them. Steps that need your identity stay with you.

**Is my data safe?** Secrets are referenced by name and never read by agents. A security summary is in [external/security-summary.md](../../external/security-summary.md).

**Does it make legal or financial decisions?** No. Legal and patent outputs are drafts for professional review. There is no financial back-end yet.

**What is its track record?** One production installation, self-reported, used as the first test case. We do not publish performance numbers yet.

**Is there a patent?** We do not comment on filings. Statements about patent status will be made only once something is filed.

**Who is behind it?** Rowan, inventor and product direction. We are looking for a business co-founder and a founding engineer.

**How can I help?** Be a design partner, introduce an operator or a cloud program, or review the open core when it is public.

**Is it related to QNFO?** QNFO is a separate project and the first test case. Ultrastable is a separate product with its own brand.
