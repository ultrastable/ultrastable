## Rules from the funding work (2026-11-10)

- Every credit program needs a live public site and domain email, so the order is: file, publish outcome-only site, apply.
- Google's top credit tier excludes angel money; it needs an institutional round.
- Don't buy paid Google Workspace on the Ultrastable domain within 31 days before the Google AI program application.
- Pitch both a Cloudflare version and a Google Cloud version (platform-agnostic core).
