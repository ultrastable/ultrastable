# Owner card: European priority filing (identity-bound steps only)

Not legal advice.

**What only Rowan can do:** (1) enter the identity values in `FORM-VALUES.md` marked bold; (2) sign the request; (3) pay the procedure fee. Everything else is prepared by the fleet.

- **Default in effect:** no filing; the specification stays private; every day before filing is novelty exposure in Europe (`DECISION-MEMO.md` section 5).
- **Why gated:** signature and payment commit the owner's identity and money; a filing date cannot be undone.
- **Prepared:** decision memo, walkthrough, fee table, form values, package checks in `ip/provisional/tools/preflight.py`.
- **Not Rowan's job:** drawing relabelling, Dutch-format PDF, claim translation draft, deadline entries.
- **Closing probe:** the filing receipt (date and application number) is recorded in `ip/evidence-log.md`, and `HANDOFF.md` lists the month-9, 12, 13 and 18 deadlines with real dates.
