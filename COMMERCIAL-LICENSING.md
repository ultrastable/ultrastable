# Commercial licensing: default terms (HYPOTHESES)

> Working draft. Every figure here is an estimate set by the virtual business manager for the owner to confirm, not a verified market price and not legal or tax advice. [ACCOUNTANT] and [LAWYER] review before any term is offered to anyone. The LICENSE does not depend on these numbers; it only says a Commercial License is a separate written agreement (LICENSE section 4).

Contact for requests: pilot@ultrastable.io (interim), subject `LICENSE`.

## Default Standard Commercial License (revenue share)

| Term | Default in effect | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue base | "Revenue" as defined in LICENSE 1.12, attributable to the licensed use | defined |
| Share | 10% of attributable Revenue | hypothesis (owner decision) |
| Free band | first USD 10,000 of attributable Revenue per year at 0%, so a first customer can test the market | hypothesis (owner decision) |
| Reporting and payment | quarterly report and payment within 30 days of quarter end | hypothesis |
| Audit | once a year on 30 days' notice, records only | hypothesis |
| Term and territory | 1 year renewing; worldwide | hypothesis |
| Fixed-fee alternative | quoted per case for companies that prefer it | hypothesis |
| Hosted operations by the licensee | same share; operator must offer source of its changes (LICENSE 5.6) | follows LICENSE |

## Other forms (LICENSE 4.2)
Public-benefit or open-source-contribution conditional licenses, time-, field- and territory-limited licenses, and custom deals, case by case.

## Process (autonomous)
1. Requests arrive at the contact above.
2. The business manager scores the request against `business/plan/`, drafts the agreement from this table, and opens one owner card (identity-bound: signing and money) with the default, the draft and a closing probe (signed copy in `business/licenses/`).
3. Nothing is granted by silence (LICENSE 4.5).

## Open decisions with a default in effect
Share and free band above; whether to offer a free time-limited evaluation license on request for companies (default: yes, by written grant, so internal evaluation is not a breach).
